## Top Picks (Score ≥ 80)

### [2607.07459] EmbodiedGen V2: An Agentic, Simulation-Ready 3D World Engine for Embodied AI
**Score: 88/100** · [arXiv:2607.07459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07459)
**Authors:** Xinjie Wang, Liu Liu, Taojun Ding, Andrew Choi, Chaodong Huang, Mengao Zhao, Ziang Li, Jackson Jiang, Chunlei Yu, Shengxiang Liu, Wei Xu, Zhizhong Su
**Code/Project:** [GitHub](https://github.com/HorizonRobotics/EmbodiedGen) · [Project Page](https://horizonrobotics.github.io/EmbodiedGen)

## TL;DR
EmbodiedGen V2 is an agentic 3D world engine that fully automates the assembly of simulation-ready environments for embodied AI — from natural-language task parsing and 3D asset generation to affordance labeling and physics-validated editing — eliminating the manual bottleneck in closed-loop policy development, with online RL on generated scenes improving real-robot task success from 21.7% to 75.0%.

## Problem
Training embodied agents at scale requires executable simulation environments: objects need physical properties, collision meshes, and interaction affordances; scenes need task-conditioned layouts, navigable topology, and cross-simulator portability. While generative 3D models can now produce visually plausible individual assets, assembling them into policy-ready task environments has remained largely manual, creating a critical bottleneck for scalable closed-loop learning. Existing pipelines treat geometry, physics, affordances, and simulator interfaces as separate post-processing steps rather than a unified representation.

## Key Contributions
1. **Unified sim-ready representation** that couples metric geometry, physical validity, interaction semantics (affordances), and cross-simulator portability (URDF, MJCF, USD) into a single data contract — providing a common interface across all generation and editing stages.
2. **Model-agnostic 3D asset pipeline** that plugs state-of-the-art generators (TRELLIS, SAM3D, Hunyuan3D) into a unified post-processing stack: quality checking, mesh repair, convex decomposition, texture baking, VLM-driven physical property recovery, and cross-simulator export. Includes a 4K+ asset collection with affordance annotations.
3. **Task-driven world and large-scale scene generation** that parses open-ended natural-language task descriptions into typed Scene Graphs, then places assets with spatial constraint solving; large-scale mode generates multi-room, traversable scenes with explicit room topology and instance-level editability.
4. **Stateful Vibe Coding agent-skill harness** — a natural-language interface that exposes the shared world state as composable, physics-validated skills, turning generation, editing, and export into bounded operations over a persistent, deployable world state.
5. **Closed-loop policy validation**: online RL purely with EmbodiedGen V2 environments improves simulation success from 9.7% → 79.8%, transferring to real robots with task success from 21.7% → 75.0%.

## Method
EmbodiedGen V2 organizes around a two-level representation. At the object level, each sim-ready asset bundles textured visual geometry, collision geometry, physical parameters (mass, friction), and affordance annotations (part-level grasps, semantic interaction attributes). At the scene level, a typed Scene Graph specifies entities, task roles (background, manipulated objects, distractors, robot), and spatial relations. The system flows through five modules: (i) sim-ready asset generation — three input types (text, unoccluded image, partially occluded image) pass through generation, geometry refinement, texture baking, VLM-driven physical property recovery, and export; (ii) affordance autolabeling — VLM-driven pipeline adds part-level interaction attributes and physically validated grasps; (iii) task-driven world generation — parses natural-language tasks into Scene Graphs, solves 6-DoF placement with spatial constraints and physics stability checking; (iv) large-scale scene generation — extends to multi-room topologies with navigable openings and instance-level editability at controllable complexity tiers; (v) Vibe Coding — a stateful agent-skill harness exposes the world representation through natural-language instructions, validating each edit before committing.

## Why It Matters
The manual assembly of simulation environments is one of the last major bottlenecks for scalable embodied AI research: generating individual 3D assets is now commoditized, but turning them into policy-ready training environments still requires expert engineering effort. EmbodiedGen V2 fully automates this pipeline with an agentic approach — 83.3% of generated task-driven worlds are directly usable for downstream simulation without manual modification. The dramatic real-robot improvement (21.7% → 75.0%) validates that the generated environments are not just visually plausible but genuinely useful for policy learning. For researchers building embodied agents, world generation systems, or scalable AI training infrastructure, this is directly actionable open-source infrastructure.
