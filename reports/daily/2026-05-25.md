## ⭐ Top Picks

### 1. Geo-Align: Video Generation Alignment via Metric Geometry Reward — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.23903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23903)  
**Authors:** Zizun Li, Haoyu Guo, Runzhe Teng, **Chunhua Shen**, Tong He  
**Category:** Video Generation → Camera Control

> The first RL framework for camera-controlled video re-rendering, breaking the dependence on scarce synchronized multi-view real data. A metric 3D estimator extracts camera trajectories from generated videos and penalizes rotation/translation deviations as a scale-aware reward. Directly on-target for your camera-controlled video work, with a paired-data-free pipeline.

### 2. One-Forcing: Towards Stable One-Step Autoregressive Video Generation — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.23458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23458)  
**Authors:** Jiaqi Feng, Justin Cui, Yuanhao Ban, **Cho-Jui Hsieh**  
**Category:** Video Generation → Autoregressive

> Pushes autoregressive video generation to a stable **one-step** regime by augmenting the DMD objective with an auxiliary GAN loss, fixing the blur of Self-Forcing and the weak dynamics of consistency distillation. Reaches VBench 83.76 — SOTA among one-step causal methods — at one-third the training cost of chunkwise models. Squarely in your top interest of autoregressive video.

### 3. CoMoGen: Controllable Motion Dynamics and Interactions with Mask-Guided Video Generation — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.22996](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22996)  
**Authors:** Adil Meric, Lin Geng Foo, Mert Kiray, Benjamin Busam, Rishabh Dabral, **Christian Theobalt**  
**Category:** Video Generation → Motion Control

> Generates interactive dynamics from a single binary mask sequence via a lightweight MaskAdapter injected into an MMDiT. Introduces a method to locate "Motion Layers" in MMDiT attention space and fine-tunes only those with LoRA — no architecture change. SOTA motion fidelity; clean, deployable motion-control recipe from Theobalt's group.

---

### 4. PiD: Fast and High-Resolution Latent Decoding with Pixel Diffusion — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.23902](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23902)  
**Authors:** Yifan Lu, Qi Wu, Jay Zhangjie Wu, Zian Wang, Huan Ling, **Sanja Fidler** et al.  
**Category:** Image Generation → Decoders

> Reformulates the latent-to-pixel decoder as conditional pixel diffusion, unifying decoding and upsampling into one generative module. Decodes 512² latents to 2048² in under 1s on an RTX 5090 (~6× faster than cascaded SR), works for both VAE and semantic (SigLIP/DINOv2) RAE latents, and DMD2-distilled to 4 steps. A practical, broadly useful component for high-res T2I from NVIDIA.

### 5. GenRecon: Bridging Generative Priors for Multi-View 3D Scene Reconstruction — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.23888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23888)  
**Authors:** Katharina Schmid, Nicolas von Lützow, Jozef Hladký, **Angela Dai**, **Matthias Nießner**  
**Category:** 3D Reconstruction → Generative Priors

> Casts multi-view RGB scene reconstruction as conditional 3D generation over overlapping chunks, lifting the object-level Trellis.2 prior to scene scale via a view-order-independent projection-based conditioning mechanism. Produces editable PBR mesh reconstructions and beats SOTA reconstruction by 16%. Strong work from the Nießner/Dai group.
