## ⭐ Top Picks

### 1. MotiMotion: Motion-Controlled Video Generation with Visual Reasoning
**Score:** 10/10  
**arXiv:** [2605.22818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22818)  
**Authors:** Lee Hsin-Ying, Hanwen Jiang, Yiqun Mei, Jing Shi, **Ming-Hsuan Yang**, **Zhixin Shu**  
**Category:** Video Generation → Motion Control

> Current motion-controlled image-to-video models rigidly follow sparse, imprecise trajectories. MotiMotion reformulates motion control as a **reasoning-then-generation** problem — a VLM first reasons about causal secondary motion (e.g., if a ball moves, surrounding objects react), then guides generation. Notable: from your collaborators MH Yang and Zhixin Shu.

### 2. Bernini: Latent Semantic Planning for Video Diffusion
**Score:** 8/10  
**arXiv:** [2605.22344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22344)  
**Authors:** Bernini Team (Chenchen Liu, Junyi Chen, Lei Li, Lu Chi, et al.)  
**Category:** Video Generation

> Unifies MLLMs and diffusion models via division of labor: MLLM does **semantic planning** (from diverse multimodal inputs), diffusion model renders pixels from that high-level plan + low-level visual features. Large team suggests this is a significant system release. Addresses the fundamental gap between language-level reasoning and pixel-level synthesis.

### 3. No Pose, No Problem in 4D: Feed-Forward Dynamic Gaussians from Unposed Multi-View Videos
**Score:** 8/10  
**arXiv:** [2605.22190](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22190)  
**Category:** 3D Reconstruction → Dynamic Scenes

> First single feed-forward model that jointly handles **dynamic content + multi-view input + unknown camera poses**. Previous methods always required at least one of those to be given. Directly relevant to real-world 3D reconstruction from casual capture.

### 4. PhysX-Omni: Unified Simulation-Ready Physical 3D Generation
**Score:** 8/10  
**arXiv:** [2605.21572](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21572)  
**Authors:** Ziang Cao, Yinghao Liu, Haitian Li, Fangzhou Hong, Zhaoxi Chen, **Ziwei Liu** et al.  
**Category:** 3D Generation

> From Ziwei Liu's lab. Unified framework for simulation-ready 3D generation across **rigid, deformable, and articulated objects** — prior methods were category-specific. Addresses physical properties alongside geometry, directly useful for robotics and digital content creation.

### 5. ForeSplat: Optimization-Aware Foresight for Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting
**Score:** 7/10  
**arXiv:** [2605.22020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22020)  
**Authors:** Yuke Li, Weihang Liu, Cheng Zhang, Yuefeng Zhang, Jiadi Cui et al.  
**Category:** 3D Reconstruction

> Feed-forward 3DGS models are trained for zero-step error, but then refined post-hoc — causing a train/test mismatch. ForeSplat trains the feed-forward network to produce **optimization-friendly initializations**, aware of the subsequent refinement. Principled solution to a well-known practical issue.
