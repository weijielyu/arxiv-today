### 90/100 · [2607.13017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13017) — FlowWAM: Optical Flow as a Unified Action Representation for World Action Models

**Authors:** Yixiang Chen, Peiyan Li, Yuan Xu, Qisen Ma, Jiabing Yang, Kai Wang, Jianhua Yang, Dong An, He Guan, Gaoteng Liu, Jianlou Si, Jun Huang, Jing Liu, Nianfeng Liu, Yan Huang, Liang Wang  
*(Institute of Automation, CAS · UCAS · FiveAges · MBZUAI · Alibaba Group)*  
[Project Page](https://flow-wam.github.io)

## TL;DR
FlowWAM adopts optical flow as a unified, video-native action representation for World Action Models, enabling a single dual-stream diffusion framework to serve both as a robot manipulation policy and an action-conditioned world model — achieving 92.94% success on RoboTwin and a state-of-the-art EWMScore of 63.71 (+18.4% trajectory accuracy) on WorldArena.

## Problem
World Action Models (WAMs) leverage pretrained video generators for robot control, but face a fundamental representation gap: numerical action tokens don't align with video generators' visual format, abstract latent codes lose spatial grounding, and frame-level visual conditions lack temporal motion structure across frames. None of these representations fully exploits the dense motion priors that pretrained video generators already encode.

## Key Contributions
1. **Flow as a video-native action representation**: Optical flow is encoded in HSV to RGB space, making it directly compatible with pretrained video generators while capturing rich per-pixel displacement across frames — closing the modality gap that plagues prior WAMs.
2. **FlowWAM dual-stream diffusion framework**: A shared pretrained video generator backbone (with only lightweight stream-specific adapters) jointly models RGB and flow streams in two operating modes — policy mode (flow → decoded actions) and world-model mode (target flow → conditioned video generation).
3. **Action-free pretraining scalability**: Since flow can be extracted from any raw egocentric video without action labels, FlowWAM leverages large-scale unlabeled video datasets for pretraining, dramatically improving data efficiency.

## Method
FlowWAM converts optical flow into HSV-encoded RGB videos that share the same format and VAE as standard RGB video streams. A shared pretrained video diffusion transformer backbone processes both streams with lightweight adapters that add only minimal parameters. During training, a two-stage strategy first pretrains on large-scale unlabeled egocentric video (flow supervision only), then fine-tunes jointly with action-labeled robot data using both video generation and action prediction losses. At inference, policy mode generates future flow sequences decoded into executable actions; world-model mode conditions video generation on target flow to predict physically plausible futures.

## Why It Matters
By unifying policy and world-model operation under a single flow-based representation, FlowWAM demonstrates that carefully chosen video-native action representations can bridge robot learning and video generation — two of the researcher's top interest areas. The scalable pretraining from unlabeled video is a key practical insight for building agentic systems that learn efficiently from internet-scale data.

### 90/100 · [2607.12042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12042) — SymbOmni: Evolving Agentic Omni Models via Symbolic Concept Learning

**Authors:** Jinxiu Liu, Jianru Li, Tanqing Kuang, Xuanming Liu, Kangfu Mei, Yandong Wen, Weiyang Liu  
*(South China University of Technology · Westlake University · Johns Hopkins University · CUHK · Shenzhen Loop Area Institute)*  
[Project Page](https://spherelab.ai/symbomni)

## TL;DR
SymbOmni solves the "perpetual novice" problem in visual generation systems — their inability to learn cumulatively across tasks — by introducing an agentic architecture that continuously abstracts experiences into reusable Symbolic Workflow Instructions through an induction-transduction cycle with verbalized backpropagation, outperforming both open-source agent systems and closed-source models (GPT-Image-1) while reducing token consumption by 40%.

## Problem
Current visual generation systems (text-to-image, text-to-video, multimodal creation) suffer from the "perpetual novice" problem: they treat each task in isolation, lack mechanisms to structure experience into reusable knowledge, and must reason from scratch every time. This leads to poor compositional generalization, brittle decision-making, and an inability to improve with use — fundamental limitations of monolithic end-to-end generative models.

## Key Contributions
1. **SymbOmni framework**: An agentic architecture for cumulative visual generation that self-improves continuously through Symbolic Concept Learning without gradient-based model fine-tuning — the first to address the perpetual novice problem directly.
2. **Symbolic Concept Box (CB)**: An optimizable memory module that abstracts low-level ComfyUI operations into reusable Symbolic Workflow Instructions (SWIs) — interpretable, composable units of successful problem-solving strategies.
3. **Induction-Transduction cycle with verbalized backpropagation**: Experiences are abstracted into symbolic concepts (induction); retrieved and composed for new tasks (transduction); refined via language-based dual-verifier feedback — enabling continuous self-improvement without parameter updates.

## Method
SymbOmni operates over ComfyUI node-based visual generation pipelines. When given a new task, it retrieves relevant SWIs from the Concept Box and composes them into an executable workflow (transduction). After execution, a dual-verifier mechanism evaluates results and generates linguistic feedback (verbalized backpropagation) that refines existing concepts or abstracts new ones into the Concept Box (induction). This cycle runs online and accumulates knowledge persistently — effectively learning without gradient descent. The system handles text-to-image, creative editing, image editing, and video synthesis tasks through the same unified mechanism.

## Why It Matters
SymbOmni demonstrates a concrete path toward self-improving generative AI systems: by externalizing knowledge into structured symbolic form and learning through language-based feedback, it achieves cumulative gains across online-learning benchmarks that monolithic models cannot match. For researchers building agentic AI and automated scientific discovery pipelines, this shows that symbolic + neural hybrid architectures can deliver measurable self-improvement in creative generation tasks — directly applicable to AI scientist systems that need to iteratively refine visual outputs.
