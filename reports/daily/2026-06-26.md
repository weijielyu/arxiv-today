### [RayPE: Ray-Space Positional Encoding for 3D-Aware Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27345)
**Score: 84/100** · arXiv: [2606.27345](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27345)
**Authors:** Minghao Yin, Jiahao Lu, Wenbo Hu, Wang Zhao, Shan Ying, Kai Han

## TL;DR
RayPE introduces a 3D-aware positional encoding for video diffusion transformers by injecting 6D Plücker ray coordinates additively into self-attention queries and keys, exploiting the algebraic equivalence between the Plücker reciprocal product and the attention dot product to achieve camera-controllable, 3D-consistent video generation with under 0.1% parameter overhead.

## Problem
Modern video DiTs (HunyuanVideo, Wan, CogVideoX) use RoPE on (u,v,t) grid coordinates, encoding the camera's sampling grid but carrying zero information about 3D scene geometry. Two tokens observing the same physical surface from different viewpoints carry no positional relationship, forcing the model to recover geometric correspondence from pixel content alone — a fundamental barrier to 3D consistency and precise camera control.

## Key Contributions
- **Plücker reciprocal product as PE basis**: The geometric relation between two camera rays is captured by the Plücker reciprocal product, which is bilinear — the same algebraic form as the Transformer dot product — allowing ray geometry to live *inside* the attention dot product rather than as an external adapter.
- **RayPE encoding**: A zero-initialized additive injection of 6D Plücker coordinates into Q and K with a query/key flip, so the symmetric-identity configuration recovers the exact Plücker reciprocal product. Attention decomposes into a content term, geometry term, and two cross-terms — ablations show all are individually necessary.
- **Normalize-Gate-Inject (NGI)**: A 3-step stabilization scheme decoupling ray direction from log-magnitude, gating the encoding, and applying PE-side RMSNorm — enabling stable training across heterogeneous pose-scale conventions (SfM, SLAM, metric depth).

## Method
RayPE is plugged into a pretrained 5B video DiT (Wan2.2-TI2V-5B) without modifying the existing RoPE. For each token, a 6D Plücker coordinate is computed from camera pose and intrinsics and projected through a small MLP head, then additively injected into Q and K with a flip. NGI decouples direction and log-magnitude, gates the encoding by the latter, and applies PE-side RMSNorm symmetric to the content branch's QKNorm. Zero initialization ensures the pretrained model is undisturbed at training start. A 4-dataset training mixture (RealEstate10K, DL3DV, PanShot, OmniWorld) is used; total parameter overhead is <0.1%.

## Why It Matters
Camera-controllable video generation is fundamental to 3D-aware image-to-video synthesis, dynamic scene generation, and world models. RayPE provides a theoretically grounded, minimally invasive solution that outperforms prior camera-conditioning adapters while fully preserving base model quality — essentially making any pretrained video DiT 3D-aware at near-zero parameter cost, with a principled geometric foundation derived from projective geometry.

### [Qwen-Image-Agent: Bridging the Context Gap in Real-World Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26907)
**Score: 82/100** · arXiv: [2606.26907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26907)
**Authors:** Zekai Zhang, Jiahao Li, Jie Zhang, Kaiyuan Gao, Kun Yan, Lihan Jiang, et al. (21 authors)

## TL;DR
Qwen-Image-Agent is a unified agentic framework for real-world image generation that bridges the "Context Gap" — the mismatch between underspecified user prompts and what T2I models actually need — by orchestrating plan, reason, search, memory, and feedback in a context-centric pipeline, outperforming strong baselines on the new IA-Bench and existing benchmarks.

## Problem
Text-to-image models are trained on fully specified prompts but deployed in real-world settings where user requests are underspecified, implicit, or require up-to-date knowledge. This 'Context Gap' — the discrepancy between user-provided context and the complete generation context required — causes T2I systems to fail on practical tasks even when their generative capability is strong.

## Key Contributions
- **Context Gap formalization**: A principled framing of T2I failure in real-world settings as a discrepancy between user context and required generation context, providing a unified lens for diagnosing and addressing these failures.
- **Qwen-Image-Agent**: A training-free, modular agentic framework integrating Context-Aware Planning (information-level, content-level, generation-level) and Context Grounding (reason, search, memory, feedback) to progressively construct complete generation context from underspecified input.
- **IA-Bench**: A benchmark covering 4 agentic image generation capabilities (Plan, Reason, Search, Memory) across 17 real-world tasks, 730 test instances, and 1801 binary checklist items with VLM-based evaluation.

## Method
The framework formalizes agentic image generation as an MDP over context states. Context-Aware Planning identifies missing information at the information level (what to acquire), content level (how to use it), and generation level (how to allocate context in multi-image/multi-turn settings). Context Grounding collects missing context via reasoning for implicit intent, web search for factual/visual references, memory for personalization and history, and feedback for iterative refinement. The resulting complete context is passed to any off-the-shelf T2I model. The entire pipeline is training-free and model-agnostic.

## Why It Matters
Agentic image generation is the emerging frontier where LLM planning meets visual synthesis for real-world deployment. Qwen-Image-Agent is the most comprehensive framework to date unifying plan, reason, search, memory, and feedback around a single principle (context gap), demonstrating state-of-the-art results and providing a public benchmark for evaluating the full spectrum of agentic image generation capabilities.

### [PhysRAG: Enhancing Physics-Awareness in Video Generation via Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26916)
**Score: 82/100** · arXiv: [2606.26916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26916)
**Authors:** Kexu Cheng, Zicheng Liu, Mingju Gao, Chunhe Song, Hao Tang

## TL;DR
PhysRAG enhances physics-aware video generation by retrieving physically relevant video exemplars from a curated database and injecting their encoded dynamics into a video diffusion transformer via learnable queries — achieving state-of-the-art physical rule compliance on PhyGenBench and VBench, with code and data to be released.

## Problem
Current video generation models (including large-scale DiTs) frequently violate physical laws because they lack explicit physical modeling: explicit physics simulators fail to generalize across diverse open-world phenomena, while data-driven implicit methods struggle with controllability and face a scarcity of high-quality, diverse physical training data.

## Key Contributions
- **PhysRAG pipeline**: A retrieval-augmented approach that retrieves physically relevant video references for a given prompt from a curated database and injects their encoded dynamics as physical priors into the video diffusion model — analogous to how humans recall similar physical experiences to predict outcomes.
- **Two-stage data filtering pipeline**: Coarse pre-filtering (caption relevance) followed by fine-grained grounded filtering (video-prompt frame consistency) reduces WISA-80K to a curated 7K high-quality diverse physics video training set.
- **Learnable query injection mechanism**: VideoMAE features from retrieved exemplars are injected into DiT blocks through learnable queries, enabling precise and controllable physical knowledge transfer without requiring explicit physical simulators.

## Method
Given a text prompt, PhysRAG retrieves the most physically relevant videos from a manually curated physical video database using semantic similarity. VideoMAE extracts spatiotemporal features from the retrieved videos, and learnable queries project these features into the DiT denoising blocks. Training uses the 7K filtered video dataset derived from WISA-80K. Evaluation on PhyGenBench (physical rule compliance) and VBench (overall video quality) shows improvement over Wan2.2-5B, PhyT2V, Pika, and Kling baselines.

## Why It Matters
Physics-aware video generation is critical for world modeling and embodied AI. PhysRAG demonstrates that retrieval augmentation — a paradigm from NLP — can effectively supply physical priors to diffusion models without explicit physics simulation, enabling generation of physically plausible dynamics (fluid, optics, mechanics) across diverse open-world scenarios. The training-free retrieval approach is also computationally efficient and generalizable.
