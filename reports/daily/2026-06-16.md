### [2606.17030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17030) — Qwen-RobotWorld Technical Report: Unifying Embodied World Modeling through Language-Conditioned Video Generation
**Score: 85/100** | Agentic AI + Video Generation

**Authors:** Jie Zhang, Xiaoyue Chen, Anzhe Chen, Chenxu Lv, Deqing Li, Gengze Zhou, Hang Yin, Haoqi Yuan, Haoyang Li, Jiahao Li et al. (Qwen Team)

## TL;DR
Qwen-RobotWorld is a language-conditioned video world model for embodied intelligence that uses a 60-layer double-stream MMDiT to predict physically grounded future visual trajectories across robotic manipulation, autonomous driving, indoor navigation, and human-to-robot transfer — ranking 1st on EWMBench and DreamGen Bench by treating natural language as a unified action interface across 20+ embodiments.

## Problem
Embodied world models typically specialize in a single domain or embodiment (manipulation only, driving only), using architecture or data choices that prevent knowledge transfer across robots. A universal model that predicts plausible future states for any embodiment using free-text action descriptions — without embodiment-specific components — does not yet exist, limiting the ability to share world priors across domains and to generate synthetic training data at scale.

## Key Contributions
- **Double-Stream MMDiT with MLLM Action Encoding**: A 60-layer double-stream diffusion transformer couples a frozen Qwen2.5-VL semantic backbone with video-VAE latents through layer-wise joint attention, with natural language as the sole action interface — enabling one model to handle 20+ embodiments and 500+ action categories without any embodiment-specific heads.
- **Embodied World Knowledge (EWK) corpus**: An 8.6M video-text dataset (200M+ frames) with action-language mappings spanning robotic manipulation, autonomous driving, indoor navigation, and human-to-robot transfer — the largest publicly described embodied world modeling dataset of its kind.
- **General+Expert Progressive Curriculum**: A two-stage training strategy — Stage 1 learns broad visual priors from general video; Stage 2 injects embodied specialization via the EWK corpus under the shared language interface — achieving state-of-the-art on EWMBench (1st), DreamGen Bench (1st), WorldModelBench, and PBench, with zero-shot generalization on RoboTwin-IF.

## Method
The architecture is a 60-layer double-stream MMDiT where one stream carries Qwen2.5-VL language-visual features (frozen) and the other carries video-VAE latents; layer-wise joint attention fuses the two streams throughout denoising. Any action is expressed as a natural-language description (e.g., "rotate wrist, grasp handle, lift"), which Qwen2.5-VL encodes into a common semantic space regardless of embodiment. Training follows a two-stage curriculum: Stage 1 trains on broad open-domain video for general visual dynamics; Stage 2 fine-tunes on the EWK corpus using action-language annotations for embodied specialization. At inference, conditioning on a current observation image and a language action prompt produces a physically plausible future video trajectory.

## Why It Matters
A universal language-conditioned world model that works across embodiments is a long-sought goal in robotics and embodied AI. Qwen-RobotWorld's key insight — that natural language is sufficiently expressive to replace modality-specific action representations — and its empirical SOTA across four benchmarks make it an important reference for anyone building embodied simulation, synthetic data pipelines, or language-guided planning systems. The EWK corpus and the general+expert curriculum pattern are independently applicable to other world modeling efforts.

### [2606.16993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16993) — DreamX-World 1.0: A General-Purpose Interactive World Model
**Score: 85/100** | Video Generation + Agentic AI | [GitHub](https://github.com/AMAP-ML/DreamX-World) · [Project Page](https://dreamx-world.github.io)

**Authors:** DreamX Team, Yancheng Bai, Rui Chen, Xiangxiang Chu, Rujing Dang, Hao Dou, Bingjie Gao, Qiwen Gu, Siyu Hong, Jiachen Lei et al.

## TL;DR
DreamX-World 1.0 is a general-purpose interactive text/image-to-video world model built on Wan2.2 that achieves real-time streaming at up to 16 FPS with precise camera control, long-horizon scene persistence via camera-geometry-based memory, and composable event control — reaching an overall score of 84.76 versus 80.79 for HY-WorldPlay 1.5 on the 5-second evaluation.

## Problem
Interactive world models require three jointly satisfied constraints that no prior system addresses together: (1) camera control must translate prescribed trajectories into geometrically consistent viewpoint changes, (2) scene persistence must maintain the layout and appearance of earlier observations even after they leave the context window, and (3) streaming generation must run at practical frame rates. Each prior system solves at most one or two of these in isolation; combining all three while preserving quality after aggressive distillation remains unsolved.

## Key Contributions
- **E-PRoPE (Efficient PRoPE)**: A lightweight variant of projective positional encoding that applies camera-aware attention to spatially reduced tokens, retaining comparable trajectory-following accuracy to standard PRoPE at approximately 30% lower inference latency — the key component enabling geometric camera control without added backbone cost.
- **Memory-Conditioned Scene Persistence**: Camera-geometry-based retrieval supplies non-local visual evidence when previously observed regions re-enter the frame; residual recycling makes the conditioning path robust to imperfect memory latents; exposure-bias mitigation trains the model on its own generated history to suppress style and color drift across autoregressive chunks.
- **Distillation + RL Alignment Pipeline**: Causal forcing + DMD-style distillation + long-rollout training converts the bidirectional video generator into a few-step autoregressive world model; a subsequent RL alignment stage with camera-control and quality rewards recovers the capabilities lost during aggressive distillation, without changing the inference interface.

## Method
DreamX-World 1.0 is initialized from Wan2.2 and trained through five progressive stages: (1) camera conditioning via E-PRoPE, (2) memory conditioning with camera-geometry retrieval and residual recycling, (3) Event Instruction Tuning for composable multi-entity event control via structured time-stamped event records, (4) autoregressive distillation using causal forcing and long student rollouts, (5) RL alignment for post-distillation quality recovery. Inference optimizations include mixed-precision DiT execution, residual reuse, 75%-pruned VAE decoding, and asynchronous pipeline parallelism — achieving up to 16 FPS on eight RTX 5090 GPUs. Training data combines Unreal Engine 5 trajectories with exact camera poses, gameplay recordings, and real-world videos with recovered camera geometry.

## Why It Matters
DreamX-World 1.0 demonstrates a practical blueprint for converting a state-of-the-art video diffusion backbone into a deployable interactive world model: each capability (camera control, memory, event control, speed) is added as an independent training stage, with RL as a final quality-recovery step. The 30%-latency savings from E-PRoPE, the memory architecture for scene persistence, and the event control framework are individually applicable components. Achieving real-time throughput (16 FPS) with all capabilities intact makes this directly relevant to simulation, gaming, and embodied AI applications that require streaming world models.

### [2606.16533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16533) — Kairos: A Native World Model Stack for Physical AI
**Score: 84/100** | Agentic AI + Video Generation | [GitHub](https://github.com/kairos-agi/kairos-sensenova) · [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/kairos-agi)

**Authors:** Kairos Team, Fei Wang, Shan You, Qiming Zhang, Tao Huang, Zuoyi Fu, Zhisheng Zheng, Yunlong Xi, Feng Lv, Xiaoming Wu et al.

## TL;DR
Kairos is a native world model stack for Physical AI that formally proves local continuation heuristics are provably insufficient for long-horizon state preservation, then addresses this with a Hybrid Linear Temporal Attention (HLTA) architecture combining gated linear, dilated sliding window, and standard sliding-window attention — trained via a three-stage cross-embodiment curriculum from open-world video through joint world-action modeling.

## Problem
World models for physical AI face four tightly coupled challenges: (1) fragmented learning from heterogeneous experience sources (open-world video, human behavioral data, robot interaction data) that are individually insufficient; (2) provably impossible long-horizon state maintenance using local-only continuation (an unacknowledged structural failure in most existing systems); (3) the gap between visual forecasting and embodied action control; (4) inability to deploy in real-time observation-action-feedback loops on practical hardware.

## Key Contributions
- **Hybrid Linear Temporal Attention (HLTA) with formal bounds**: Three-level temporal memory — standard sliding-window (local dynamics), dilated sliding windows (mid-range dependencies), gated linear attention with gated delta updates (persistent global memory) — with formal theoretical proof that this temporal factorization strictly limits error accumulation and mathematically guarantees state propagation across extended horizons, unlike pure local-continuation models.
- **Native Pre-training Paradigm with Cross-Embodiment Data Curriculum**: A three-stage developmental pathway — Stage I (physical pretraining on open-world video for broad visual priors), Stage II (embodied pretraining with human-centric data for behavioral priors), Stage III (joint world-action training for embodied control grounding) — that unifies heterogeneous experience sources into a single model without embodiment-specific components.
- **Deployment-Aware System Co-Design**: Timestep distillation, hardware-aware inference optimization (server-grade and consumer-grade code paths), and self-evolution + prompt self-alignment at inference time enable real-time closed-loop operation and continual adaptation.

## Method
The core architectural contribution is HLTA, which processes temporal sequences with three attention types operating at different scales: local sliding-window attention handles frame-level dynamics; dilated sliding-window attention with increasing dilation factors captures mid-range temporal dependencies; gated linear attention with gated delta updates maintains a compressed persistent global state across the full sequence. The formal theoretical analysis establishes (a) necessity — pure local continuation heuristics incur irreducible excess risk that grows with horizon; (b) approximate sufficiency — HLTA's hybrid design bounds this risk, with the global-memory component providing provably limited error propagation. Training follows the three-stage curriculum that progressively refines the model from broad visual knowledge to embodied action grounding.

## Why It Matters
Kairos provides both a theoretical foundation and a practical system for building world models that work as infrastructure for physical AI rather than demonstration videos. The formal proof that local continuation fails for long-horizon state maintenance is a significant conceptual contribution — it explains why many prior world models appear strong on short benchmarks but fail in deployment. The complete stack (training curriculum, efficient architecture with theoretical guarantees, deployment-aware inference) positions Kairos as a replicable recipe for the next generation of physically grounded world models, with open code and model weights available.
