### [E³C: Video Generation with 3D Environmental Memory and Ego-Exo Human Pose Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26316) — Score: 91

**Authors:** Qiao Gu, Lingni Ma, Adam W Harley, Richard Newcombe, Florian Shkurti, Julian Straub

**TL;DR:** Controllable egocentric video diffusion conditioned on a semi-dense point cloud 3D environmental memory (with appearance features) plus explicit ego and exo human pose controls, enabling geometrically grounded and human-motion-adherent egocentric video synthesis.

**Problem:** Generating controllable egocentric video is exceptionally difficult: the camera couples tightly to the actor, causing rapid viewpoint changes and frequent self-occlusions. Existing models lack persistent 3D spatial memory and do not jointly handle the ego wearer's articulated body (often partially out of view) and surrounding exocentric people.

**Key Contributions:**
- Semi-dense point cloud 3D environmental memory built from context frames, augmented with video-VAE appearance descriptors and rendered into the target camera view as a persistent structural condition
- Ego pose encoder converting 3D body joints and 6DoF wrist poses into persistent cross-attention tokens (not drawn onto frames), preserving control under self-occlusion
- Exo human pose control via rendered 2D skeleton overlays from YOLO-X/ViTPose, jointly conditioned alongside the 3D memory
- Disentangled conditioning enables intuitive scene editing (removing objects from 3D memory, changing surrounding people's motion) without retraining

**Method:** Built on Wan2.1-VACE-1.3B, LoRA fine-tuned on the Nymeria egocentric dataset. Constructs a semi-dense point cloud from 200 context frames via Project Aria MPS, augments each point with VAE appearance features, renders into target camera views. Ego poses encoded into cross-attention tokens via a temporal transformer with gated fusion.

**Results:** On Nymeria: FVD 249 vs. next-best 328; PSNR 18.6 vs. 17.3; TErr 2.40 cm vs. 3.25 cm; Hand-F1 41.44 vs. 34.24. LPIPS 17.3 vs. 24.3 (EgoControl); camera error 2.40 cm vs. EgoTwin's 67.0 cm.

**Why It Matters:** Advances controllable human video generation for embodied settings. The disentanglement of persistent scene structure from human-driven dynamics and the pose tokenization approach for self-occlusion offer transferable design patterns for systems jointly controlling camera trajectories and articulated human motion.

### [LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26486) — Score: 87

**Authors:** Meituan LongCat Team, Xunliang Cai, Meng Cheng, Feng Gao, et al.

**TL;DR:** Open-source audio-driven human avatar video generation from Meituan achieving commercial-grade quality through data engineering, per-frame RLHF (GRPO), and DMD2 distillation to 8 NFEs, matching or surpassing HeyGen and OmniHuman 1.5.

**Problem:** Audio-driven avatar methods that perform well on benchmarks fail in production: they lack long-horizon temporal stability, identity consistency, robust lip sync across diverse speaking styles, and ability to handle multi-person interactions, stylized characters, and object handling.

**Key Contributions:**
- Audio encoder upgraded from Wav2Vec2 (94M) to Whisper-large (1.5B, trained on 680k hours) for tighter lip synchronization
- Per-frame GRPO: extends video-level RLHF to temporally fine-grained credit assignment, reducing hand/face distortion
- Parameter-efficient DMD2 distillation: single DiT backbone with interchangeable Generator and Fake Score LoRA adapters, compressing 150 NFEs to 8 NFEs
- L-RoPE multi-person generation binding each speaker region to its audio condition; silent audio tracks for background characters

**Method:** DiT-based 3D video diffusion (3D VAE, 3D self-attention, Whisper-large audio cross-attention). Three training stages: base training (flow matching, low-res to 720p), GRPO post-training (per-frame rewards, multi-clip rollout up to 5 clips), DMD2 distillation (dynamically mounted LoRA adapters).

**Results:** On 508 image-audio pairs evaluated by 770 crowdsourced raters + 10 experts: wins pairwise A/B against HeyGen, OmniHuman 1.5, and Kling Avatar 2.0. 8-NFE model shows lower rationality issue rate (32.4%) than 50-step base (51.5%).

**Why It Matters:** Demonstrates that a carefully engineered open-source system can match proprietary commercial avatars. The per-frame GRPO formulation and parameter-efficient DMD2 distillation generalize to other long-video diffusion systems.

### [MRT: Masked Region Transformer for Layered Image Generation and Editing at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27235) — Score: 86

**Authors:** Zhicong Tang, Zhao Zhang, Jingye Chen, Mohan Zhou, Yifan Pu, Yuchi Liu, Yalong Bai, Ethan Smith, Yuhui Yuan

**TL;DR:** 20B-parameter masked region diffusion model for multi-layer transparent image generation and editing, trained on 10M multilingual design samples, unifying text-to-layers, image-to-layers, and layers-to-layers in one framework with 108x speedup over prior art.

**Problem:** Layered image generation (producing independent RGBA transparent layers) is underexplored at scale. Prior approaches lack overflow layer support, scale compute linearly with layer count, or don't unify generation and editing tasks.

**Key Contributions:**
- Unified masked region diffusion: three tasks (T2L, I2L, L2L) via selective token masking — masked clean tokens as conditioning, noised tokens denoised
- Overflow-aware canvas layer representation for layers extending beyond the visible canvas (>60% of real designs)
- DMD + CacheDiT + sequence parallelization: 3-6s inference on 1-4x H100/H200 (108.5x speedup, 23.6x memory reduction vs. Qwen-Image-Layered)
- Layer grouping augmentation: randomly merges overlapping layers during training for robustness

**Method:** Full-parameter fine-tuned Qwen-Image (60 layers, hidden dim 3584), trained in two progressive stages at 512x512 then 1024x1024 on 64x H200. Layout bounding boxes from a z-order-aware detector spatially constrain each layer's generation region.

**Results:** PSNR_merged 27.34 vs. 25.81 (Qwen-Image-Layered) for 4-8 layer bucket. User study: MRT wins 79.5%/68.9%/82.6% for layer quality/integrity/granularity over Qwen-Image-Layered; preferred over Lovart, RoboNeo, and other commercial systems.

**Why It Matters:** Layered image synthesis at scale enables workflows where generated assets are immediately decomposable and re-editable. Open-sourced 20B-parameter model trained on 10M multilingual real-world design data.

### [VDE: Training-Free Accelerating Rectified Flow Model via Velocity Decomposition and Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23381) — Score: 86

**Authors:** Junwen Tan, Jinglin Liang, Hongyuan Chen, Shuangping Huang

**TL;DR:** Replaces static feature caching with velocity decomposition into scalar-direction form, exploiting temporal regularity of each component to estimate velocity at skipped steps without a forward pass — 2-3x speedup on FLUX.1 and Wan2.1 while outperforming all cache-based baselines on quality. (Cross-list from cs.AI)

**Problem:** All training-free acceleration methods for rectified flow models (FLUX, Wan) cache intermediate features from a previous timestep and reuse them unchanged. As the denoising input evolves, stale cached features create growing mismatch, degrading quality especially at high acceleration ratios.

**Key Contributions:**
- Velocity decomposed as v_t = a_t*x_t + b_t*||x_t||*u_t (parallel and orthogonal components relative to input x_t)
- Empirical discovery: scalar coefficients evolve with strong local linearity; orthogonal direction u_t is stable (cosine similarity ~0.99 between adjacent steps)
- Estimation: linearly extrapolate coefficients from recent anchors; reuse stable u_t; reconstruct velocity from the current input — input-adaptive, not stale
- Training-free, plug-and-play across image, video, and 3D generation

**Method:** Periodic anchor steps do full forward passes and decompose velocity. Between anchors, VDE linearly extrapolates scalar coefficients, reuses the orthogonal direction, and reconstructs velocity with the actual current input, eliminating static-cache mismatch.

**Results:**
- FLUX.1 [dev]: 2.21x speedup, LPIPS 0.1243 vs. 0.3197 (best cache baseline)
- FLUX.1 fast: 3.01x speedup, SSIM 0.8267 vs. 0.7240 (baseline)
- Wan2.1 video: 2.08-2.50x speedup, VBench near baseline, LPIPS 52% better than baselines

**Why It Matters:** A new acceleration paradigm for rectified flow DiTs (FLUX, Wan) that outperforms all existing caching methods. The velocity decomposition insight may generalize to inform why certain techniques work on specific model families — directly relevant to the agentic acceleration research project.

### [PARE: Pruning and Adaptive Routing for Efficient Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27336) — Score: 85

**Authors:** Yutong Wang, Yunke Wang, Tianfan Xue, Yu Qiao, Yaohui Wang, Xinyuan Chen, Chang Xu

**TL;DR:** Jointly prunes video DiT width and depth with spatial-temporal-aware pruning and input-adaptive block routing, achieving ~52% parameter reduction and ~50x total speedup on Wan2.1-14B with minimal quality loss.

**Problem:** Video DiTs like Wan2.1-14B require enormous compute. Prior compression applies static architectures uniformly, ignoring that temporal attention heads are critical but have smaller activations, and that block importance varies per sample and timestep.

**Key Contributions:**
- Spatial-temporal aware width pruning: classifies heads into spatial/temporal types, applies 1.5x protection multiplier to temporal heads; diversity-aware FFN neuron selection
- Content-adaptive block router: lightweight MLP conditioned on sinusoidal timestep + visual content (CLIP I2V; latent statistics T2V), top-K gating with linear budget schedule (more blocks at high-noise steps)
- Progressive 3-stage training: width distillation, joint width-routing, optional step distillation
- ~50x total speedup: ~2x from pruning/routing, 12.5x from 50 to 4 steps, 2x from CFG removal

**Method:** WANDA-style importance scores with timestep weighting and temporal head protection; diversity-filtered greedy FFN selection. Block router: 3-layer MLP mapping [SinEmb(t); e_c] to block-wise logits with straight-through top-K gating.

**Results:**
- Wan2.1-T2V-14B: 52% parameter reduction, VBench 77.10 vs. teacher 77.70; beats NeoDragon (74.68), F3-Pruning (69.84)
- Wan2.1-I2V-14B: 52% reduction, VBench 76.24 vs. teacher 77.92
- With 4-step distillation: VBench ~77.00 T2V, ~75.93 I2V at 45% reduction

**Why It Matters:** Most comprehensive compression study on Wan2.1-scale video DiTs. Spatial-temporal head specialization and input-adaptive routing are directly actionable for the agentic acceleration research project.
