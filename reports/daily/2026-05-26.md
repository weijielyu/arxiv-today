## ⭐ Top Picks

### 1. On-Policy Adversarial Flow Distillation for Autoregressive Video Generation — 9/10
**arXiv:** [2605.26105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26105)  
**Authors:** Yang Luo, Shengju Qian, Xiaohang Tang, Zirui Zhu, Yong Liu, Xin Wang, **Yang You**  
**Category:** Video Generation → Autoregressive

> Tackles the hard problem of distilling strong black-box teachers into causal autoregressive students, where the student must learn under its own rollout distribution and SFT is off-policy. AFD rolls out the student on shared prompts, trains a prompt-paired Bradley-Terry discriminator to estimate clean-sample teacher-student discrepancy, and converts it into a denoising-time on-policy signal. Squarely on your top interest of autoregressive video, with a principled fix for the streaming/long-horizon distillation bottleneck.

### 2. DeltaCam: Differential Intrinsic Camera Modeling for Video Generation — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.25266](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25266)  
**Authors:** Debabrata Mandal, Zhihan Peng, Yujie Wang, Praneeth Chakravarthula  
**Category:** Video Generation → Camera Control

> Goes beyond extrinsic pose control to make *intrinsic* camera behavior — depth-of-field, exposure, lens distortion, color processing — controllable and temporally consistent. Introduces Δ-parameterized neural camera adaptors that operate on relative changes, sidestepping the lack of large-scale absolute-intrinsics video data. Directly relevant to your camera-controlled video work, opening a new and under-explored control axis.

### 3. Helix4D: Complex 4D Mesh Generation — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.26109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26109)  
**Authors:** Jiraphon Yenphraphai, Jianqi Chen, Jian Wang, Gordon Qian, **Sergey Tulyakov**, Rameen Abdal, Raymond A. Yeh, **Peter Wonka**, Chaoyang Wang  
**Category:** 3D Generation → 4D

> Adapts the Trellis2 image-to-3D prior into video-conditioned 4D mesh generation, handling complex topology changes, transparent materials, thin structures, and inner surfaces that current video-to-4D methods fail on. Uses sliding-window cross-frame attention anchored on a Trellis2-generated first frame to inherit pretrained quality on rare cases, plus a temporal injection that preserves the 3D positional encoding. Strong dynamic-3D generation work from Snap Research.

---

### 4. TriSplat: Simulation-Ready Feed-Forward 3D Scene Reconstruction — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.26115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26115)  
**Authors:** Weijie Wang, Zimu Li, Jinchuan Shi, Zeyu Zhang, Botao Ye, **Marc Pollefeys**, Donny Y. Chen, **Bohan Zhuang**  
**Category:** 3D Reconstruction → Feed-Forward

> Replaces Gaussian primitives with oriented triangle primitives so a single feed-forward pass directly exports simulation-ready meshes — no expensive post-hoc surface extraction that breaks the feed-forward promise. Jointly predicts point maps, triangle attributes, camera poses, and optional intrinsics in a pose-free setting. A clean, practical advance in feed-forward sparse-view reconstruction from the Pollefeys/Zhuang circle.

---

### 5. Full-4D: Generating Full-Scope 4D Scenes from a Single-View Video — 8/10
**arXiv:** [2605.25500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25500)  
**Authors:** Tingxi Chen, Ke Hao, Yabo Chen, Zhengxue Cheng, Rong Xie, Li Song, Haibin Huang, Chi Zhang, **Xuelong Li**  
**Category:** 3D Generation → 4D

> Casts ill-posed single-view-to-4D as multi-view video synthesis followed by optimization-based 4D reconstruction, escaping the small-viewpoint-perturbation limits of prior monocular methods. Backs it with Real-MV-4D, a new large-scale dataset of synchronized multi-view videos of full-scope dynamic scenes. A compelling video→4D pipeline bridging your video-generation and 3D interests.
