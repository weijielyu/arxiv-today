## Top Picks (Score ≥ 80)

### 82/100 · [2607.11886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11886) — Read It Back: Pretrained MLLMs Are Zero-Shot Reward Models for Text-to-Image Generation

**Authors:** Runhui Huang, Qihui Zhang, Zhe Liu, Yu Gao, Jie Wu, Hengshuang Zhao  
*(University of Hong Kong, ByteDance Seed, Peking University)*  
[Project Page](https://huangrh99.github.io/SpectraReward/)

## TL;DR
SpectraReward turns any pretrained MLLM into a training-free reward model for T2I generation RL by measuring how well the original prompt can be "read back" from a generated image via a single teacher-forced forward pass — no preference labels, no fine-tuning needed. The self-rewarding variant (Self-SpectraReward) shows that a UMM's own understanding branch can match or outperform reward models 4–30× larger.

## Problem
Reward model design is the bottleneck of image generation RL: human preference annotation is expensive, zero-shot MLLM judges are noisy and calibration-sensitive, and question-decomposition pipelines are complex to engineer. No principled, preference-free, off-the-shelf reward signal existed for open-source image generators.

## Key Contributions
1. **SpectraReward**: A training-free reward function that conditions a frozen MLLM on a generated image and performs one teacher-forced forward pass over the prompt, using mean prompt-token log-likelihood as the reward — directly reusing the MLLM's pretrained image-text alignment with zero additional training or preference data.
2. **Self-SpectraReward**: For unified multimodal models, the policy's own understanding branch serves as the reward model for its generation branch, forming a closed-loop self-improvement framework entirely free of external models or knowledge.
3. **Comprehensive RL study** across 2 generator backbones (SD3.5-M, BAGEL), 3 RL algorithms, 9 reward MLLM backbones from 4 families (4B–235B params), and 5 out-of-distribution benchmarks — showing Self-SpectraReward outperforms all external MLLM reward models on GenEval and TIIF-Bench.

## Method
Given a generated image and the original text prompt, SpectraReward conditions a frozen pretrained MLLM on the image and performs a single teacher-forced forward pass over the prompt tokens, obtaining per-token log-likelihoods. The mean of this "semantic spectrum" is used directly as a scalar reward for RL training (GRPO/DancePO/FlowGRPO). Self-SpectraReward specializes this to unified multimodal models (e.g., BAGEL), where the understanding branch shares the same tokenizer and vision encoder as the generation branch — this distributional alignment makes the self-reward particularly effective, outperforming much larger external reward models.

## Why It Matters
This work sidesteps both the cost of preference annotation and the noise of scalar MLLM judging, suggesting that the prompt-likelihood metric is a principled and practically accessible reward signal. The key finding — that reward-policy distributional alignment matters as much as reward model scale — is a concrete and actionable insight for anyone building image generation RL pipelines or automated generation systems. The closed-loop Self-SpectraReward framework is especially compelling as a scalable self-improvement paradigm.
